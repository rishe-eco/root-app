# J12 — the customer dashboard and a home screen per staff role

**Built:** 2026-10-05 · **Plan:** `root-sot/ecosystem/working/root-studio-journeys-build-plan.md` §5 "J12" (its trap: derived, never stored); §6.3 attention kinds; journeys Q3
**Branch:** `stage-j12-home` — built by a Sonnet lane (`54f8266` → `bcc4dc6`), reviewed by Opus (`8a56bbe`). No migration.

`/app` is now the customer's **Dashboard**: what is waiting on them, then what is waiting on Root, each linking to the place where it gets done. The desk's home is **My work**: what is waiting on this staff member, by capability and assignment. Both come from one query, `myAttention`, which runs the contract page's own attention rules across every contract the viewer may see. Nothing new is stored.

## 1 · What was built

- **`myAttention`** (`resolvers/home.ts`, pure rules in `lib/home.ts`):
  - **Customer:** the attention module across all their published contracts, plus their pending requests, open tickets, and contract-less invoices (subscriptions, service runs). It runs the same subscription catch-up as `myInvoices`.
  - **Staff:** contracts admitted by `contractVisibleToStaff`, narrowed by `mayWork` to items whose job the viewer holds:
    - sales: `contracts.author`, plus `feedback.approveAnswers` for pending answers and `billing.manage` for invoices;
    - design: `canAuthorDesign`;
    - development: `canAuthorBuild`;
    - ADMIN: everything, through `assignments.override`.
  - **Delivered contracts** leave designer and developer homes through J11's `isActiveContract`.
  - **No second visibility rule:** each contract goes through `attentionFor` with the viewer, so `attentionVisibleTo` still hides internal and money items.
  - **Destinations:** every item carries a `route` without the language prefix (`routeFor`, a table pinned in `home.test.ts`). The portal wizard and the desk Steps tab honour `?step=&phase=`.
- **Cost:** a fixed **32 statements** for a customer, sales and ADMIN alike, whether there are 2 contracts or 29. `j12.test.ts` counts them through `statementsRun()`, a query-event counter in `lib/prisma.ts` that is off in production.
  - **Shared loader:** `loadFactsMany` is the wizard's batched loader (15 `IN` queries). The contract page calls it with a list of one.
  - **Shared banner rule:** `Contract.pending` is split into `pendingNeeds` and `pendingOf`, so the banner and the home use one rule.
- **New attention kinds (§6.3):**
  - `STEP_WAITING`: the design, mockup or a phase's demo is current and nothing has been shown. It is the designer's or developer's move.
  - `ITERATION_PUBLISHED`: closes J9b's gap.
  - `DEMO_READY_TO_APPROVE` and `DEMO_NOT_READY`: J10 declared both but never emitted them. `DEMO_NOT_READY` is internal.
  - `INVOICE_DUE`: one line per invoice.
  - `INVOICE_ISSUABLE`: one line per contract with a count, internal and money.
  - `REQUEST_MESSAGE` and `TICKET_REPLY`: computed in `lib/home.ts`, because they belong to no contract. The turn is "whose message was last", an ownership edge (`authorId === customerId`).
  - `DRAFT_TO_REVIEW` gains the variants `draft` (the first published draft on the new road) and `sign` (approved, not signed).
- **Role changes:** `INVOICE_OVERDUE` now has role SALES, who follows it up. A technical `COMMITMENT_OVERDUE` has role DEVELOPER.
- **Mockup status:** `nextMoveOn` for MOCKUP now reads like the design step. Before, it always said Root's.
- **De-duplication:** a published mockup or demo line is suppressed while a note is open, while a declared fate is in an unpublished build, or when an `ANSWER_PUBLISHED` line already covers that step.
- **Portal:**
  - The rail reads Dashboard, Contracts, Invoices, Tools, Support.
  - Standing actions: new request, open the current contract, open a tool.
  - Three honest empty states: nothing yet, all clear, and only Root's move.
  - **Customers now land on `/app` after signing in** (Opus review).
- **Desk:**
  - `DeskHome` sends every staff member to **My work** (`/desk/work`).
  - Overview needs `contracts.readAll`.
  - V4's Needs-Root queue is re-pointed from `ContractStatus` to the attention module. It now returns `NeedsRootEntry { contract, since, count }`. The tiles still count the enum.

## 2 · Decided

- **No read record.** Every kind clears through the next act of whoever's move it is, never because someone looked. `PROPOSAL_DECIDED` stays unemitted because it would need a read log. `ALLOWANCE_EXHAUSTED` and `ASSIGNEE_INACTIVE` are also left out.
- **`role` is the job that follows an item up, not whose move it is.** For example, an overdue invoice waits on the customer and is sales' to chase.
- **Mixed accounts:**
  - The staff loop skips the viewer's own contracts.
  - The Dashboard shows only `CUSTOMER`-audience items and My work only `STAFF` ones (Opus review). Before this, a staff member opening the portal saw their desk's lines on the Dashboard.

## 3 · Owed
