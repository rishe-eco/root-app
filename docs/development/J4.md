Step 1 of the new road exists: sales writes the brief (what kind of site, which template type, design tier, budget cap, deadlines, who provides the domain, hosting and content, support, allowances), the extras checklist, the phases with their planned end dates and the payment plan; the brief's commitments become rows on the project's dependency board; `publishContract` hands step 1 to the customer, who reads it in the wizard and comments on any field. **Phases now belong to a contract**, which retires every "the project's first contract owns the work" stopgap J2–S2 left.

## 3 · Decided

1. `designTier` is required: a brief always has kind, type and tier, so `BRIEF_MISSING` means "no brief row".
2. `setBrief` is a replace, not a patch — an omitted field is cleared.
3. **Complete** (`briefCompleteness`, REQUIREMENTS "done") = budget, hard deadline, the three providers, support months, the allowance matching the design path, demo rounds; every implied commitment dated; at least one phase, each dated; a payment plan that exists and, once there is a fee, adds up to it. `idealDeadline` and `domainName` are never required.
4. Commitments are per project (`briefKey` unique per project); a sibling contract's brief adopts the same row. A key no brief implies any more is deleted unless verified — a verified row is detached and kept. An undated commitment is not created and shows as `COMMITMENT_UNDATED`. Unknown keys are ignored. `gateway`, `smsLine`, `enamad` default to CUSTOMER.
5. Commitment due dates are stored as midnight UTC (`Dependency.dueAt` stays a timestamp) and rendered with `calendarDate`.
6. `updatePhase` distinguishes an omitted `milestoneLabel` from null.
7. A REQUIREMENTS `briefField` must be a known field (`INVALID_TARGET`), mirrored on the web and pinned by a test.
8. Step panels stay on the step they opened on, so a finished brief does not swap its own form for SCOPE mid-use.
9. Small S2 fix: `DemoStepPanel` passes `asCustomer`, so view-as-customer no longer asks for feedback as staff.
10. Persian: customer strings use «تو» (brand §5), desk strings stay formal.

## 4 · Owed

- **J13:** `setPaymentPlan` replaces `PlannedInvoice` rows wholesale. Once `BillingEntry` links to them, a replace must refuse or preserve linked rows.
- **Amendment bodies** (`Amendment.bodyFa/En`) can carry money in prose and are not behind `mayReadArticleBody`. Neither the plan nor J4 names them.
- **No desk UI for `Article.sensitive`** — the API arg exists; the web `Article` fragment does not select it.
- **A `contracts.author` without `money.read`** would clear `budgetCap` on save (`setBrief` is a replace). No role has that split; the form hides the budget and plan for them.
- `Project.phases` and `Project.progress` still span every contract on the project.
- `createContract` still makes only legacy contracts; the e2e seeds a draft new-road contract (`RC-2026-022`) for the hand-over.
