On the new road the contract's scope is **seeded from the brief's template type**; the customer proposes additions, removals and restorations in their own words; sales confirms each (writing the bilingual label) or declines it with a reason; "confirmed" is derived — no undecided proposal. Once scope is confirmed and the brief complete, sales publishes the draft; the customer leaves notes per article, each showing its fate and whether its article changed in a later revision. The customer half of a scope trade exists, so a trade can finally execute.

## 2 · Decided

1. **No sales assignee exists** (S1 assigns designer and developer only), so a proposal notifies every active `contracts.author` holder.
2. **`SCOPE_ALREADY_SEEDED` is per contract:** a second contract on a project seeds its own items; a template key already taken on the project gets the next suffix (`home.2`), the kind decided from the plain key, the project row locked while seeding. This also pays **J2's owed `applyContractTemplate` on a second contract**.
3. `applyContractTemplate` on `AGREEMENT_FIRST` writes articles only — the fixed six-item list would seed a second scope beside the type's.
4. `removable` = `AGREED` only; later states are a trade or an amendment. Extras are never proposable (`NOT_PROPOSABLE`).
5. `decideScopeProposal` takes optional `key` and `kind`; extra codes `KEY_TAKEN`, `ITEM_STATE_CHANGED`, `ALREADY_DECIDED`, `PROPOSAL_PENDING`; the customer's text becomes the item's `originNote`.
6. **Publish gate order:** `CONTRACT_SIGNED` → `STEP_LOCKED` → `SCOPE_UNCONFIRMED` (via `scopeStepDone`, so an unseeded contract refuses too — the same function as the wizard signal) → `BRIEF_INCOMPLETE` (with `extensions.missing`) → `NO_CHANGES`. `DRAFT_PUBLISHED` goes out only on the new road and only once step 1 was handed over.
7. **`articleChangedSince`** means the content changed; a pure renumbering reports `currentArticleNumber`. "The same article" is title (either language) then number; same number with a different title is `articleGone`, never guessed.
8. **Planned dates:** no stage said who writes them — `setPlannedDates` added. A customer now reads them from the first published draft (Q12, "proposed in the draft") rather than only after approval; `statusSummary` follows. J7 freezes them at approval.
9. `FeedbackPanel` gained `onAnswered` so the DRAFT "done" state re-derives without a reload.
10. `executeScopeTrade`'s gate is unchanged (both confirmations since L1); `confirmScopeTradeCustomer` is deliberately **not** under `assertStepOpenOn` — trades are the post-lock path.
11. **Step locks:** every J5 write, `publishContractRevision`, `applyContractTemplate` and `setPlannedDates` take `lockContract` + `assertStepOpenOn(SCOPE | DRAFT)`. **`setArticle`, `updateContractDraft`, `deleteArticle` are left to J7.**
12. `{{client}}` is `clientName` (else the customer's name), one string in both languages — an English body can read "استودیو نهال (the Client)". `{{provider}}` is «ریشه» / Root.
