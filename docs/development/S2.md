# S2 — one feedback model, the §3.1 rule, answers and the approval queue

**Built:** 2026-09-29/30 · **Plan:** `root-sot/ecosystem/working/root-studio-journeys-build-plan.md` §5 "S2", PD-1–PD-4, PD-21; journeys §3.1, §7.10
**Branch:** `stage-s2-feedback` — built by a Sonnet lane (`4d819aa` → `9458aa8`), reviewed by Opus

Every step's feedback is now **one model with one lifecycle** (PD-1): the customer raises; Root answers — directly, or through a sales approval when the answer matrix says so (PD-3); Root resolves; the customer may reopen until the step is approved; approval accepts everything and locks the step, for staff too. This rewrites L3's `FeedbackItem`, L3b's build queue and L4's conversion — the widest change in the plan, cheap only because production holds no data (P0-1).

## 1 · What was built

- **Migration `20260929180000_s2_feedback_everywhere`:** `FeedbackItem` gains `projectId`, `contractId`, `step`, `phaseId` (backfilled — the demo's project, the project's primary contract, `DEMO`, the demo's phase), a nullable `demoId`, and the anchor columns for every step; **one CHECK, `FeedbackItem_anchor_matches_step`**, holds which anchor each step may carry; duplicate collapse per anchor (plain uniques where Prisma can say it, four hand-written partial indexes where NULL-distinctness would defeat it — the two `general` ones and two whole-revision ones); `FeedbackStatus` drops `RATIFIED` and gains `MOVED`; `FeedbackComment` becomes the answer (resolution, approval state, approver, note, original body) with CHECK `FeedbackComment_declined_has_reason` (whitespace-only refused too). Rehearsed on a scratch database seeded with legacy rows, `RATIFIED` included.
- **`lib/feedbackRules.ts`:** `answerRoute`, `reopensOnComment` (ADDRESSED, DECLINED, MOVED reopen — the §3.1 reversal of L3b §2.5), `stepBlockers`, `acceptanceOnApproval`, `answerGoverns`, `anchorMatchesStep`, `isMoneyAnchored`. **`lib/stepApproval.ts`:** `approveStepTx` — the one approval J7–J10 call, under a contract row lock; the only writer of `acceptedAt` and `StepApproval`, held by a brace-matched source scan. **`lib/visibility.ts`:** `visibleComment` (a pending or declined answer is invisible to the customer — and under view-as), `visibleItem` (PD-21: money-anchored items hidden from staff without `money.read`).
- **API:** `submitFeedback(contractId, step, phaseId, target, …)` (the contract's customer only; `STEP_LOCKED`; ratified at submission — PD-2), `answerFeedback`, `approveAnswer` / `declineAnswer`, `approveStep`, `pendingAnswers`, `myFeedbackQueue`, `Contract.feedbackItems`. **Retired:** `ratifyFeedback`, `acceptFeedback`, `RATIFIED`. `declareBuild`'s dispositions are the developer's direct answers, scoped to `step: DEMO` and the phase; `createTicketFromFeedback` sets the item `MOVED`.
- **Web:** a generic `FeedbackPanel` (the demo panel a thin wrapper), the wizard's DEMO step panel with the customer's approve button (J10 replaces it), the desk **Approvals** queue (`feedback.approveAnswers`).
- **Notifications:** customer feedback → the step's owner; an answer pending → approvers; published → the customer (one per mutation); declined → its author.

## 2 · Decided

1. **`approveStep` approves DESIGN, MOCKUP and DEMO only** (`STEP_NOT_APPROVABLE` otherwise; `DESIGN_FIRST` refused) — with no J7 gate yet, a bare AGREEMENT approval would let a customer lock steps 1–3. J7–J10 open their own steps through `approveStepTx` with their preconditions.
2. **`answerGoverns`:** approving an old pending answer applies its resolution only if no later visible resolving answer and no later customer comment exists; otherwise it publishes the words and leaves the state.
3. **J3's owed `addressedInBuild`:** `Build.declaredBy` and `.changes` resolve; a customer sees the builder's name (contacts stay behind `mayReadContactOf`) and no raw change entries (L3b §1.3 keeps no-origin notes staff-only).
4. `ratifiedAt/By` are kept as "who raised it", out of the SDL.
5. DEMO and MOCKUP feedback on a non-primary contract refuses `STEP_NOT_ON_ROAD` until J4 puts a contract on each phase.

## 3 · Changed expectations in L3 / L3b / L4 (PD-2)

L3: submissions use the new signature; the ratification test is now "submission is its own ratification" and asserts the retired mutations are gone from the schema; staff submitting gets `NOT_FOUND` (the contract's customer only). L3b: no ratified fixture; the second write is `approveStep` (not `acceptFeedback`); `NOT_ADDRESSED` → `STEP_HAS_OPEN_FEEDBACK`; an open item takes any disposition; a disposition is a direct comment the customer sees. L4: converts an OPEN item and asserts `MOVED`; `NOT_RATIFIED` → `NOT_OPEN`. J3: direct inserts gain the new columns; the `addressedInBuild` scalars-only hold is removed.
