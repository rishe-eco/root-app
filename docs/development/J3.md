# J3 — steps, sequence, the wizard shell, the status area, attention, internal notes, view-as-customer

**Built:** 2026-09-29 · **Plan:** `root-sot/ecosystem/working/root-studio-journeys-build-plan.md` §5 "J3", PD-7, PD-17, PD-19, §6.2–6.3
**Branch:** `stage-j3-wizard` — built by a Sonnet lane (`e819760` → `abe859a`), reviewed by Opus

The contract becomes a wizard whose steps are derived from state; a status area sits above it; one computation says what is waiting on whom; staff get a private notes thread; sales can see exactly what the customer sees. **This is the backbone J4–J12 plug into** — every shell below names its extension points in its own header comment.

## 1 · What was built

- **Migration `20260929160000_j3_steps`:** `ContractSequence` (existing rows `DESIGN_FIRST` — the column is added with that default, then the default flips to `AGREEMENT_FIRST`), `StepKind`, `StepApproval` with **two hand-written partial unique indexes** (`phaseId IS NULL` / `IS NOT NULL` — not `NULLS NOT DISTINCT`, which needs Postgres 15), `plannedStartAt` / `plannedEndAt` as `@db.Date` (PD-19; the plan wrote `DateTime`), `InternalNote`.
- **Pure rules:** `lib/steps.ts` (`stepsFor`, `computeWizard`, `assertStepOpen` → `STEP_LOCKED`), `lib/attention.ts` (the whole §6.3 catalogue declared; four kinds emitted today — `DRAFT_TO_REVIEW`, `ROUND_PUBLISHED`, `COMMITMENT_OVERDUE`, `ANSWER_PUBLISHED`), `lib/statusSummary.ts`, `lib/timeline.ts` (PD-17), `lib/internalNotes.ts`.
- **View as customer:** `contract(id, asCustomer: true)` sets `ctx.viewAs` to the contract's customer; every field resolver asks `viewerOf(ctx)` — **there is no `asCustomer` branch anywhere**, so the view cannot drift from the customer's own read. A field-by-field parity test compares the two. `NOT_VISIBLE_TO_CUSTOMER` for a contract the customer cannot see yet.
- **API:** `Contract.wizard`, `statusSummary`, `attention`, `timeline`, `internalNotes` over one loader; `addInternalNote`; `assertStepOpenOn` (the database-backed check).
- **Web:** shared `StepList`, `StatusArea`, `StepPanels` (the panel registry — its header lists J4–J11, one line each), `Timeline`, `ViewAsBanner`; the portal's new-road `Wizard`; the legacy `ContractDetail` unchanged beneath a status area; the desk workspace gains **Steps** and **Notes** tabs; the desk contracts list shows the current step and whose move it is. The seed adds `RC-2026-021`, an `AGREEMENT_FIRST` contract on the seeded customer's project.

## 2 · Decided

1. **`createContract` still creates `DESIGN_FIRST`** — an `AGREEMENT_FIRST` contract cannot be signed until J7, and `04-workspace` plus many integration files take an API-created contract to signature. The column default is the plan's; the resolver passes `SEQUENCE_FOR_NEW_CONTRACTS` from `lib/steps.ts`. **J4 flips that one line** (a tripwire unit test marks it).
2. **`assertStepOpen` is called from nowhere yet** — nothing writes a `StepApproval` before J7. The mutations it will guard (the draft in `admin/contracts.ts`, the registry, all of `admin/design.ts`) are listed in `assertStepOpenOn`'s header.
3. **Phases, demos and builds belong to the project's first contract until J4 adds `Phase.contractId`** (`ownsProjectWork`); a second contract shows a placeholder demo step.
4. **Planned dates reach the customer only after the AGREEMENT approval** (`mayReadPlannedDates`) — they are proposed in the draft and frozen at approval.

## 3 · Found on review (Opus)

- **Staff contact details reached customers.** `Contract.designer` and `developer` (and every other edge that returns a `User` — comment authors among them) carried the staff member's email and phone to the customer. `User.email` / `User.phone` now resolve through `mayReadContactOf` (`lib/visibility.ts`): the account itself, contract readers, and whoever holds `customers.manage`, `staff.manage` or `review.admin`. Checked on the field, so a later edge cannot reopen it; under view-as the customer's projection hides them too.

## 4 · Owed / noted

- **`FeedbackItem.addressedInBuild`** loads a bare `Build`, so selecting `Build.declaredBy` or `.changes` through it fails. Nothing selects them today; S2 reworks feedback and fixes or removes the edge.
- `Contract.statusSummary` costs about ten queries per contract on the desk list — fine at today's size; batch if the book grows.
