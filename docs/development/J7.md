## 2 · Decided

1. **`REVISION_STALE`:** approval locks the live rows but the signature stands against the snapshot; format 2 carries every step 1–2 row, so "the steps moved since publish" is refused — publish again.
2. **Trap 1 held:** `agreementView` withholds the budget and the plan only from staff `mayReadMoney` does not admit; the customer's step 3 and the print route carry everything, sensitive article bodies included (tested against the designer).
3. The snapshot freezes the template type as slug and labels; commitments (`Dependency` rows) are **not** in it — board state that lives after signature. The appendix is this contract's items plus the project's extras, so a sibling contract's items never enter a document the customer signs.
4. **Approve and sign are the contract's own customer's** on the new road (legacy unchanged).
5. **"Signed" stays the signature** (J13's rule); AGREEMENT is *done* at signature and *locked* at approval.
6. `Amendment.sensitive` is a column, not derived from `relatesToArticle` — an amendment adding a paid extra relates to no article. `issueAmendment` defaults it from the related article; re-pointing at a sensitive article never un-hides it.
7. **Review fix — the registry's status door:** the lane locked add/update/delete but left `setScopeItemStatus` open, so an agreed item could be declined after signature. A status change **into or out of `DECLINED`** now goes through the same SCOPE lock (`inOpenScope`); every other move (AGREED → IN_BUILD → IN_DEMO → ACCEPTED) stays open. A row in the lock table and a test that the lifecycle still moves after the lock.
