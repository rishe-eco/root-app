Once the agreement is signed, the design step opens. On the **pre-made** path the designer proposes rounds of catalogue themes; the customer browses a gallery (the preview in-page, the vendor's demo in a new tab — never framed), asks for other options or chooses one. On the **custom** path the designer uploads per-page wireframes and a palette through iterations; the customer approves each page. A round counts against the brief's allowance; sales can extend it, optionally billed. Approving the design locks it.

## 2 · Decided

1. **The design opens only once the agreement is signed** (`AGREEMENT_NOT_SIGNED`, via J13's `agreementSignedAt`); legacy unaffected.
2. **One approval writer:** `approveDesign` is the DESIGN approval; S2's `approveStep(DESIGN)` now refuses `STEP_NOT_APPROVABLE`, as for AGREEMENT. Completeness — pre-made: a concept chosen; custom: at least one page, all approved, a non-empty palette. No brief → `BRIEF_MISSING`.
3. The custom path has one implicit concept, `wireframes`, created by `startDesignDraft`; it is never "chosen" (`chooseConcept` → `NOT_APPLICABLE`).
4. **"Show me other options"** is S2's general DESIGN item marked `otherOptionsAskedAt` (the general thread is one per step, so the ask needs its own marker); the next publish answers it `ADDRESSED` through `answerRoute` (F9) and clears the marker. Pre-made only.
5. `proposeThemes` is a replace; kept themes keep their snapshot and copy; a dropped theme's copy is released only when nothing references it.
6. Palette rules: colour `#rgb`/`#rrggbb` stored as lowercase six digits; font names letters, digits, space and `.-_`, 60 characters, no CSS syntax; logo note 280 characters; role `[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}` unique per kind; both labels; at most 24 items; custom path only (`NOT_CUSTOM`).
7. `extendAllowance` is DESIGN only (`STEP_NOT_BUILT` for MOCKUP/DEMO until J9b/J10); the charge amount follows `mayReadMoney`, the reason is staff-only.
8. An unset allowance means no limit (unreachable on a complete brief); legacy contracts have none.
9. **Theme demos are never framed** — the plan's trap over its own web paragraph, and T2's decision.
