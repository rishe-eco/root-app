# J11 — step 7: the summary, handover, closing a contract

**Built:** 2026-10-01/04 · **Plan:** `root-sot/ecosystem/working/root-studio-journeys-build-plan.md` §5 "J11", PD-6, PD-17, PD-19, Q-S8
**Branch:** `stage-j11-summary` — built by a Sonnet lane (`6d4606f` → `712a679`, paused 2026-10-02 → 10-04 for the founder's weekly limit), reviewed by Opus. **Built before J10:** a phase's acceptance is produced in tests by inserting the DEMO `StepApproval` that J10's `approveDemo` will write.

A record, not a review. Deliverables are handed over one by one with a note; sales closes the contract once every phase is accepted and every agreed deliverable handed over; the contract then leaves every `readActive` view. The customer gets the **summary** — what was agreed, what became of each item, the iterations each step used against its allowance, planned against actual dates, invoices, the support period — printable, with no feedback in it (Q-S8).

## 1 · What was built

- **Migration `20261002120000_j11_delivery`:** `ScopeItem.handedOverAt / handedOverById / handoverNote`, `Contract.deliveredAt / deliveredById` (PD-6); CHECK `ScopeItem_handover_is_a_deliverable` — a handover needs kind `DELIVERABLE` and a note with a non-space character (`~ '[^[:space:]]'`, and `IS NOT NULL` explicitly), proven by direct insert (a FEATURE; a note of tabs and newlines).
- **`isActiveContract` gains a third clause:** delivered means not active — on `deliveredAt`, not `status = DONE` (status is hand-settable). Every select feeding the visibility functions carries `deliveredAt`; `buildPhaseScope` and technical-dependency verification filter it.
- **Libs:** `lib/delivery.ts` (close blockers, `assertCanClose`, the note check, `supportPeriod`), `lib/summary.ts` (end states, executed amendments, unprompted-change visibility, per-step rows, invoice totals); `CONTRACT_CLOSED` → the customer (email + SMS, both locales); timeline `CONTRACT_DELIVERED`.
- **API (`resolvers/delivery.ts`):** `markHandedOver` (`contracts.author`, or `builds.author` **and** the contract's developer — capability and edge separately), `unmarkHandedOver` (`contracts.author` only, before the close), `closeContract` (`contracts.author`, under `lockContract`; refusals in order `ALREADY_DELIVERED`, `CONTRACT_DISCARDED`, `CONTRACT_NOT_SIGNED`, `PHASES_NOT_ACCEPTED`, `DELIVERABLES_PENDING`), `Contract.delivery` (what blocks the close — one function for the desk's button and the refusal), `Contract.summary` (null until signed).
- **Web:** the wizard's SUMMARY panel (`SummaryPanel` — for staff, the handover form and a two-step Close), `SummaryDocument` (shared with the print route `/app/contracts/:id/summary/print`), a summary link on a delivered legacy contract.

## 2 · Decided

1. A second `markHandedOver` is `ALREADY_HANDED_OVER` — a note is never rewritten in place, so "who handed it over" stays true.
2. **Legacy contracts close too:** a legacy phase is accepted when all its counted items are `ACCEPTED` (the wizard's `isPhaseAccepted`). A contract with no phases cannot be closed on either road.
3. "Agreed deliverable" is the set Appendix 1 freezes (kind `DELIVERABLE`, this contract, agreed or beyond) — a sibling contract's never blocks.
4. Unprompted changes: a customer sees only those written up in both languages; staff see all, with the raw note (L3b §1.3). Published builds only, SITE and MOCKUP.
5. **Iterations per step** come from `lib/allowance.ts` (`roundCountsOn`, `iterationsUsed`, `allowanceFor`) unchanged — DEMO reads "not counted" until J10 fills it; `summary.test.ts` proves the summary follows when it does.
6. Money (`mayReadMoney`) on invoice totals and the agreed plan; totals are issued invoices only. Dates are calendar days in UTC (PD-19); `support.until` clamps to a shorter month's last day.
